Project details

NATO Strategic Simulator

Independent educational project

Overview

An independently published educational simulator that turns strategic and institutional decision-making into two interactive scenarios. It focuses on uncertainty, competing priorities and consequences rather than factual recall.

My contribution

I researched the relevant processes, designed and wrote the scenarios, built the interface in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, implemented branching decisions, indicators and report outputs, tested the experience and deployed it for desktop and mobile.

The project

Users choose a scenario, work through briefings and decision points, and receive cumulative indicators, short analysis and evolving report outputs. The scenarios are fictional composites informed by public sources and practitioner review where needed.
